# Read, predict, execute, explain

Two university courses supply complementary foundations: Berkeley CS267 for parallel machines, communication and load balance; MIT 6.5840 for distributed-system failure and MapReduce. Neither substitutes for the pinned Spark 3.5.8 implementation guides. Choose these relevant units instead of treating an entire graduate course as a prerequisite for this introductory Lab.

The reviewed catalogue below is searchable in both languages and downloadable as structured JSON. Each learning unit names a particular lecture, paper section or assignment specification and a separate original runnable exercise. Predict the result before running, verify the stated invariant, then explain the observed plan and counters. The mapping does not grant credit or reproduce a university assignment solution.

Verification date: 14 November 2026, Asia/Shanghai. Published material is distinguished from tentative schedules. The CS267 2016 archive is deliberately historical; its hardware figures are not 2026 capacity advice. The 2025 official page says its videos require a Berkeley login.
